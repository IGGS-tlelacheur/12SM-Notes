Introduction to Differential Equations

A differential equation is an equation that involves a function and its derivative(s). The solution to the equation is the function itself.

The order of a differential equation is its highest derivative and the degree is the highest power of the highest derivative.

Differential equations are used to model many real-life situations including velocity, force, temperature change, and population growth.

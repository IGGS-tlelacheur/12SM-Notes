  1. The heights of a particular species of plant are normally distributed with a mean of 45 cm and a standard deviation of 6 cm. A random sample of 9 plants is selected and their mean height calculated. What are the mean and standard deviation of this sample mean?

  2. The time taken to complete a task is normally distributed with a mean of 52 minutes and a standard deviation of 8 minutes. A random sample of 16 workers is selected.
    (a) Find the probability that a randomly selected worker takes more than 56 minutes.
    (b) Find the probability that the mean time for the 16 workers is more than 56 minutes.
    (c) Compare your answers to parts (a) and (b).

  3. The weights of apples from a particular orchard are normally distributed with a mean of 180 g and a standard deviation of 25 g.
    (a) Find the probability that a randomly selected apple weighs less than 170 g.
    (b) Find the probability that the mean weight of 20 randomly selected apples is less than 170 g.

  4. The blood pressure readings of adults in a certain population are normally distributed with mean mmHg and standard deviation mmHg. What is the probability that a random sample of 25 adults will have a mean blood pressure reading between 115 and 125 mmHg?

  5. The lengths of bolts produced by a machine are normally distributed with mean 5.00 cm and standard deviation 0.05 cm. A quality control inspector selects 10 bolts each hour. What is the probability that the average length of these 10 bolts differs from the target of 5.00 cm by more than 0.02 cm?

  6. Boxes of cereal are filled by a machine. The amount of cereal in each box is normally distributed with mean 502 g and standard deviation 4 g. A random sample of 8 boxes is selected. Find the probability that the average weight of cereal in these 8 boxes is less than 500 g.

  7. The reaction times of drivers to a particular stimulus are normally distributed with mean 0.8 seconds and standard deviation 0.12 seconds. In a study, the reaction times of 36 drivers are measured. What is the probability that the mean reaction time of this sample is greater than 0.85 seconds?

  8. The daily rainfall in a certain region during summer is normally distributed with mean 2.5 mm and standard deviation 1.2 mm. Over a 30-day period, what is the probability that the average daily rainfall exceeds 3.0 mm?

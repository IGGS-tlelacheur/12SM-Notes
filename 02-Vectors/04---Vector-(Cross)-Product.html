Vector (Cross) Product

The vector product (or cross product) of two vectors in 3D space produces a vector result.

The vector product of two vectors is a vector at right angles to both the original vectors. Its magnitude is the product of the magnitudes of the original vectors and the sine of the angle between their directions. This property makes the cross product essential for finding normals to planes.

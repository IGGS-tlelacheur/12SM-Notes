Rectilinear Motion

Definitions

Kinematics is the study of the movement of objects.

We are only concerned with motion in a straight line, which is known as rectilinear motion.

In Mathematics we treat all objects as points or dimensionless particles.

Displacement

Displacement measures the change in position of an object relative to a fixed point (the origin).
